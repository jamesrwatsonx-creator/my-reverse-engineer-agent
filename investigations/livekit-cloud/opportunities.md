# Gods Eye View — strategic opportunity and staged build

## Thesis (INFERRED)
The voice receptionist is a compelling entry wedge. The durable differentiator is the verified, permissioned directory of local businesses and human-owned agents together with outcomes/availability and agent-to-agent coordination. Business reach, reliable delegated actions and distribution are stronger moats than any single LLM.

## Pilot wedge
Start with a geographic density strategy (e.g. Windsor-area service businesses), not a global map requiring users everywhere. Offer missed-call recovery, actionable follow-up, appointment/quote/request capture and direct engagement. Support non-appointment businesses from day one through configurable service outcomes.

## Sequence
- **V0, infrastructure spike:** one real web voice agent, one SIP number, LiveKit dispatch, Hermes persistent agent context, TTS, interruptions, one test session report.
- **V1, paid business receptionist:** owner onboarding + polished greeting demo; CRM-lite call inbox/transcript/summary, leads, quote/support/booking workflows; built-in scheduling plus Google/Microsoft connection; consent/logging; business landing page, handle; simple skills/agent settings.
- **V1.5, personal agent:** friends/family greeting rules; 'text me if' alerts, tasks, next-day planner, opt-in morning briefing + prep calls; protected contacts and permissions. Keep personal data isolated.
- **V2, Gods Eye View density:** licensed business listing ingestion, neutral unclaimed pins and handles, claim workflows, public capability manifests, service/category/map search, first ambassador program with verified claims and quality review.
- **V3, agent communication:** Telegram-like inbox for human/agent/business, A2A-compatible discovery/task handling, explicit consent/approval, booking between agents, referrals and dispatch.
- **V4, extensibility:** curated skills gallery -> vetted paid marketplace, business campaign composer + opt-in referral credits/attribution, developer SDK + third-party agent gateway.
- **V5, optional ownership layers:** portable verifiable claims, wallet/payments/payouts, ownership/control attestation, property registration interface only with legally credible evidence, token programs subject to law and genuine customer demand.

## Important corrections
1. World ID verifies uniqueness of a human, not business owner or land title; combine with business-domain, official phone/business registry/company authorization and disputes; GPS is supporting evidence only.
2. For map seeding, avoid unlawfully rehosting Google Places content; use appropriately licensed open POI sources or first-party import.
3. Android system-level assistant invocation is device/OS constrained; use app/shortcut/widget and test default assistant support separately. Apple via App Intents/Shortcuts first.
4. MCP = connect agent to tools/services. A2A = communication and task coordination among agents. Both require authorization.
5. Transcripts/recordings, location, voice templates and marketing create compliance and abuse risk; include retention/deletion, transparency, privacy by design, and consent history early.
6. Human-approval policy for third-party outreach, financial commitments, cancellations and irreversible actions is mandatory.
7. An unclaimed pin is a directory record, not an authorized AI representative.

## Validation gates
- Successful unattended inbound call with useful structured outcome.
- Booking completed with no duplicates under concurrent booking attempts.
- Reliable summary and alert with attribution to actual call transcript.
- Customer can opt out of recording and marketing independently.
- Business owner can claim/reject/dispute listing; agent doesn't impersonate unclaimed business.
- Personal agent never leaks private memory to business clients or public search.
- Measurable business benefit: missed calls recovered, qualified leads, confirmed bookings, owner hours saved, retention and willingness to pay.
